Artificial intelligence has revolutionized how we interact with technology. Machine learning, a subset of AI, enables computers to learn patterns from data without explicit programming. Deep learning, using neural networks with multiple layers, has achieved remarkable results in image recognition, natural language processing, and game playing. These technologies are now embedded in everyday applications from smartphone assistants to recommendation systems. The future of AI promises even more sophisticated capabilities as computational power increases and algorithms improve. However, ethical considerations around bias, privacy, and job displacement remain important challenges to address.
